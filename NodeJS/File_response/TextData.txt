
National & Political News

• Anti-CEC Protests & Supreme Court Plea: Opposition leaders led by Rahul Gandhi filed a plea in the Supreme Court demanding a full disclosure of records, agendas, and notings related to the SIR process—which has resulted in the deletion of roughly 13 crore voters nationwide—while protests and detentions continued around Delhi.
• L Murugan Resignation: Union Minister of State L Murugan wrote to Prime Minister Narendra Modi asking to step down from the Union cabinet and relinquish his Rajya Sabha seat to work at the grassroots level in Tamil Nadu.
• Stock Market Rally: Sensex surged over 1,100 points, boosting investor wealth by over ₹4.12 lakh crore, driven by IT stocks, a stronger rupee, and reactions to finance minister remarks on Long-Term Capital Gain (LTCG) tax.
• MEAs Ladakh Stance: The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) rejected China's objections regarding the naming of places in Ladakh, firmly reiterating that Ladakh is an inalienable and integral part of India.
